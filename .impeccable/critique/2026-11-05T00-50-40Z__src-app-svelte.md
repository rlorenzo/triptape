## Design Specificity Verdict

Design review: weak, because only the copy is specific to TripTape. The visuals are stock Tailwind: gray boxes, a blue button and a black nav pill. A keepsake product has no keepsake feel.

Detector: the source scan of `src` and `index.html` found nothing; the problems only show up in the browser. Overlay findings: Import 1 low-contrast placeholder (preview-only input); Library 1 low-contrast placeholder; Story 17 low-contrast at 3.9:1 (12 chips, 5 placeholders), 12 labels at 10px, 12 nested cards, 1 line of about 152 characters; Export and Settings none. Likely false positives: nested cards (intentional thumbnail tiles), and white-on-white hits in light mode from near-black buttons that are actually about 16:1.

## Overall Impression

The structure and honesty are good, but the app feels like a database admin tool at the moment it should feel like getting your vacation back. The biggest opportunity is the ending: the parent waits through a render and gets a list of filenames.

## What's Working

- Import: one headline, one action, and a plain consent note that appears once.
- Reels disclosure: inline and non-blocking, with no checkbox, as `PRODUCT.md` requires.
- Honest states: Settings says its controls aren't wired up, and Story edits save automatically.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] Dark mode makes the app unusable. Nav labels and card headings render white on white; only the active tab is readable. `src/app.css:4` declares `light dark`, but surfaces are hard-coded light. Fix: remove `dark` for now, and add a real dark theme only if wanted. Command: /impeccable harden
2. [P1] No path forward and a flat finish. Library and Story have no Continue button, and Export ends in a bulleted file list and a Dismiss button (`Export.svelte:83-88`). Fix: one primary next step per screen, and a completion view with Play film and Show in folder (check both against the backend). Command: /impeccable shape
3. [P1] Accessibility falls short of the `PLAN.md` requirements. Export progress has no `progressbar` role or live region; consent has `role="dialog"` but no focus move or Escape handling (`Import.svelte:85`); Import and Export errors have no `role="alert"`; focus doesn't move on a screen change; Story has 3.9:1 chips and 10px labels. Command: /impeccable audit
4. [P2] Story reads as a form with many decisions. Every photo carries a caption field, Replace and Remove, under a monospace ID. Fix: identify photos by thumbnail and caption, show one chapter at a time, reveal editing on selection, and lead with a "looks good" path. Command: /impeccable distill
5. [P2] Library has a dead filter and no trip structure. `best` is identical to `all` (`Library.svelte:9`), and the grid has no day or place groups. Fix: remove `best` until scores exist, and group by day and place. Command: /impeccable layout
